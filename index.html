✔ Official Responsible Disclosure Acknowledgment
📧 Subject: Responsible Disclosure Acknowledgment Dear Shayan,

Thank you for taking the time to conduct security testing on the TaskPlanet platform and for sharing your findings with us in such a detailed and responsible manner.

We sincerely appreciate your efforts in identifying and reporting these vulnerabilities. Your report has helped us strengthen our platform's security, improve our transaction integrity, and address potential risks across several critical areas.

We are pleased to acknowledge your report as a Responsible Disclosure.

We would also like to confirm that our team has completed the necessary fixes for the reported vulnerabilities. The updates include improvements to:

• Reward redemption: Strengthened one-time reward validation and transaction handling to prevent duplicate claims.
• Points and coin conversion: Implemented safeguards to ensure balances are validated and updated correctly during concurrent transactions.
• Payment and subscription verification: Improved payment validation to prevent unauthorized activation of Premium, Premium Plus, and Pro subscriptions.
• Administrative and moderator access: Strengthened role-based access controls to ensure privileged features are accessible only to authorized users.
• User data protection: Improved authorization checks on user APIs to prevent unauthorized access to sensitive information.
• Authentication security: Updated login validation to ensure credentials are properly verified and cannot be bypassed using exposed password hashes.
• Input validation: Added stricter validation for payment amounts and other transaction inputs to prevent negative-value manipulation and unauthorized point generation.

We are happy to see security researchers taking an active interest in the safety and reliability of TaskPlanet. Responsible disclosures like yours play an important role in helping us build a more secure and trustworthy platform for our users.

We encourage you to continue exploring TaskPlanet and reporting any additional security vulnerabilities you may identify. Our team values constructive security research and will be glad to review further findings you wish to share.

Thank you once again for your contribution and for helping us improve TaskPlanet. — Himanshu Singh · Founder, TaskPlanet 📅 Received: September 2025
